Transform the existing Case Files game into a tournament-based workplace mystery game.

GOAL

The game should feel like a mix of:

- Kahoot
- Jackbox
- Among Us
- Office Gossip

The game should be fun, simple, visual, and discussion-focused.

The objective is not deep detective work.

The objective is team discussion, voting, surprises, and laughter.

====================================================
TOURNAMENT MODE
====================================================

Support:

- 2 to 5 teams
- 6 to 10 players per team
- 30 to 50 total players

All teams play the SAME case each round.

After each round:
- Reveal culprit
- Award points
- Update leaderboard

At the end:
- Display winning team

====================================================
CLUE DESIGN RULES
====================================================

Clues may reference:

- Roles
- Responsibilities
- Expertise
- Access Levels
- Habits
- Opportunities
- Character Names

Names may be mentioned directly.

However:

A clue should not directly reveal the culprit.

Good clues create discussion.

Good clues create suspicion.

Good clues leave room for multiple interpretations.

Players should debate.

Bad clues immediately reveal the answer.

====================================================
EXAMPLE CLUES
====================================================

"Prince Boni suggested the tracker needed major improvements."

"Nori Ocampus noticed something unusual earlier that morning."

"The action required elevated access."

"Bekz Sarap was reviewing reports generated from the tracker."

"The dashboard was moved, not deleted."

"The incident involved a workflow normally reviewed by Nori."

====================================================

Every player votes.

No team captain voting.

====================================================
TIE HANDLING
====================================================

If there is a tie:

Show tied suspects only.

Run a quick 30-second runoff vote.

====================================================
VOTING TIMER
====================================================

Voting timer:

60 seconds

Host configurable.

When timer expires:

Use only submitted votes.

Missing votes do not block progress.

UI DESIGN
