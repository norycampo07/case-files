CASE 01 — THE MISSING COFFEE MUG

Family:
Coffee

Difficulty:
Easy

====================================================

BRIEF

A beloved coffee mug vanished shortly after lunch.
The owner insists it is irreplaceable.
The investigation has already become far more serious than the mug deserves.

====================================================

CORE CLUES

Clue 1

The mug disappeared shortly after a project meeting ended.
Only someone involved in post-meeting activities would likely have handled it.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 2

The mug was not thrown away.
Whoever moved it probably believed they were putting it somewhere useful.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 3

Several people entered the pantry after lunch.
Only one role regularly volunteers to organize shared spaces.

====================================================

OPTIONAL CLUES

Clue 4

Several items in the pantry were found in new locations.
Most people did not notice.
The mug did.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 5

One team member stayed behind after the meeting to help organize the room.
Several objects were relocated.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 6

Prince Boni never entered the pantry that afternoon.
It did not stop everyone from accusing him.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 7

Andy Iglesias and Bekz Sarap were overheard having a hushed conversation in the pantry.
Several investigators immediately became suspicious.
The conversation concerned a recent breakup.
Neither had coffee. Only spilling tea.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 8

A cabinet was reorganized that afternoon.
The person responsible maintains that the new system is objectively better.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 9

Nori Ocampus remembers seeing the missing mug after the meeting ended.
The mug vanished later.

----------------------------------------------------

Clue 10

A distinctive sticker from the missing mug was found near a storage cabinet.
The mug appears to have been moved rather than stolen.

====================================================

CULPRIT

Peri Vasquez

====================================================

REVEAL

The mug was not stolen.
While helping clean up after the meeting, Peri Vasquez reorganized several pantry cabinets.
The missing mug was placed in a new location that made perfect sense to Peri.
After all, Peri usually knows exactly where documents, approvals, and trackers should be moved.
Unfortunately, that confidence extended to coffee mugs.
The mug spent the afternoon exactly where Peri believed it belonged.
Nobody else agreed.

- two recommendations
- and three additional concerns
